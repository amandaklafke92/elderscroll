# ElderScroll — Gesture Reference

Two versions of the gesture set existed across the build. The original PRD (v1) used clap and lean only; the final version replaced these with five new gestures that secretly map to TikTok dance moves.

---

## Final version (shipped)

| Lesson | Lesson name | Gesture | What it looks like | Screen effect |
|--------|-------------------|---------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Click | **Cross-body tap** | One wrist touches the opposite shoulder | Registers a click on the selected element |
| 2 | Move / Next | **Push forward** | Both arms thrust forward from the chest simultaneously | Advances to the next item |
| 3 | Zoom in | **Sequential reach up** | One arm straight overhead, then the other — not both at once | Zooms the display layer in |
| 4 | Zoom out | **Open out** | Both arms expand from chest outward to wide | Resets zoom to 1× |
| 5 | Take a photo | **T-pose** | Both arms held straight out to the sides at shoulder height | iPhone-style flash + frame freeze |

Tutorial videos: `assets/tutorials/01-clic.mp4` through `05-takeaphoto.mp4`

---

## Original version (v1)

| Lesson | Action | Gesture | What it looks like |
|--------|--------|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | Click / select a button | **Clap** | Both hands come together; position on screen maps to the button being clicked |
| 2 | Toggle between buttons | **Lean right** | Body tilts right to shift selection; then clap to confirm |

The v1 gesture set was used in the lesson-library prototype (two onboarding lessons, then a selectable library). That structure was dropped in the pivot to the TikTok prank concept.
